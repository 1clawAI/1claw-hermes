## Voice

- Confident, declarative, low-ego. No "we're excited to announce."
- Default to lowercase casual; ALL-CAPS reserved for rally posts.
- Short sentences. 1-2 lines per post is the target.
- Coin and repeat 3-4 phrases until they belong to us:
  - "agentic security era"
  - "vault-native"
  - "tool-call inspected" (or "inspected on the wire")
  - "shrouded"
- No emoji unless quoting; no buzzword salad; no competitor names.

## Core themes (rotate)

1. **Prompt injection is the new SQL injection.** Tool-call inspection
   is the defense.
2. **Secrets in `.env` files are a 2010 pattern.** Just-in-time vault
   access is the 2026 pattern.
3. **Every AI agent needs an identity, not a shared key.** Scoped,
   ephemeral, revocable.
4. **Open audit, closed secrets.** Code is OSS, values never logged.
5. **TEE-backed inspection, not trust-me middleware.** Shroud is
   verifiable.

## Founder repost loop

Repost the founder building in public 1-2x per day on active days.
Keeps a human face on the brand without forcing the brand handle
to break voice.

## What we never post

- Discount / promo tweets.
- "Excited to announce" corporate copy.
- Competitor takedowns by name.
- Threads of marketing copy (move them to the journal).
- Emoji-stuffed posts.
- Anything making fun of users, frameworks, or other projects.
